# SayWords 0.40.3

- Public builds use colorful Lucide icons for general words. Brand and product names remain text. Dictated text, pasted text, and history retain the original words.
- Pause dictation, edit the transcript, then resume or finish and paste. The capsule grows upward while controls stay in place.
- The dictionary separates manual and learned terms, offers suggestions from corrections or explicitly pasted text, and supports optional acceptance of repeated corrections. Automatic acceptance is off by default.
- Live captions have an explicit enable switch. They stay off until enabled.
- Report a bug or suggest a feature from General settings. No diagnostics are sent automatically.

Apple Silicon; macOS 14 or later. On-device translation requires supported hardware and macOS 26 or later. Intel is not included in this package. Microphone, physical shortcuts, destination-specific paste, and full-screen/multiple-display behavior have not all been re-tested in this release.

Automatic update checks are off by default; enable them in Settings or check manually. Existing preferences and vocabulary keep the same app identity. Lucide ISC and included Feather MIT notices are bundled with the app.
